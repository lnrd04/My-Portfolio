OUR PROJECT'S

Titan Foundation was born from a legacy of large-scale construction excellence. Since 2016, projects delivered with precision and integrity have grown into a foundation dedicated to advancing resilient infrastructure, training future tradespeople, and supporting communities impacted by construction development. Titan Foundation combines deep industry expertise with a public-minded mission: to build stronger places and stronger people.

FINISHED PROJECTS

Teju Tower

Established in 1989, Teju Tower began as a visionary project meant to redefine urban architecture. What started as a small design experiment has since become a landmark of progress, symbolizing innovation, resilience, and the city’s relentless pursuit of growth.

Thomas Gonzalez

Completed in 1995, the Thomas Gonzales Building rose as a beacon of ingenuity and vision. Crafted to merge elegance with efficiency, it quickly attracted pioneering businesses and creative ventures. Today, it symbolizes progress, resilience, and a commitment to excellence that continues to inspire.
